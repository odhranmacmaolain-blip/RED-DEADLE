How to Play Red-Deadle

1. Outlaw Guess (5,000 pts): Identify the RDR2 character in 6 tries. 5,000 (1 try), 4,000 (2–4 tries), 3,000 (5 tries), 1,500 (6 tries).

2. Story Pinpoint (5,000 pts): Drop a pin on the RDR2 map for 5 story or stranger mission prompts (up to 1,000 pts each). Place names are hidden until after you lock in your pin.

3. Lore Dossier (1,500 pts): Unlocks after completing Mode 1 in Daily mode (always open in Practice mode). Answer 3 trivia questions about the target outlaw at 500 pts each.

Spoiler Protection: Days #1–#50 strictly feature pre-Chapter 5 characters and story events.
