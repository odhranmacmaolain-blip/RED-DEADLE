How to Play Red-Deadle

1. Outlaw Guess (Up to 5,000 pts): Deduce the daily RDR2 character within 6 guesses. Scoring: 5,000 (1 try), 4,000 (2–4 tries), 3,000 (5 tries), 1,500 (6 tries).

2. Story Pinpoint (Up to 5,000 pts): Pinpoint 5 iconic RDR2 story moments, gang heists, character introductions, or stranger encounters on the frontier map (up to 1,000 pts per round based on accuracy).

3. Bounty Dossier (Up to 1,500 pts): Unlocks immediately after you finish today's Outlaw Guess. Answer 3 multiple-choice lore questions (500 pts each) about today's character.
